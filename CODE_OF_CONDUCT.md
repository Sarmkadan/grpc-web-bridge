# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all people who engage with gRPC-Web Bridge. We do not tolerate behavior that is disrespectful to our community members or that excludes, intimidates, or causes discomfort to others.

## Our Standards

We expect all members of the gRPC-Web Bridge community to conduct themselves professionally and respectfully. This includes:

### Positive Behavior
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing opinions and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members
- Helping others succeed and learn
- Providing constructive feedback

### Unacceptable Behavior
- The use of sexualized language or imagery
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting
- Discrimination based on age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation
- Violence or threats of violence
- Unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Deliberate intimidation
- Stalking or following
- Deliberate misgendering or use of 'dead' or rejected names
- Pattern of inappropriate social contact, such as requesting/assuming inappropriate levels of intimacy with others

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:
- All project repositories
- Issue trackers and pull requests
- Project discussions
- Project events
- Any communication channels used by the community

This Code of Conduct applies both within project spaces and in public spaces when an individual is representing the project or its community.

## Enforcement

### Reporting Violations

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please report it by emailing the project maintainers at [contact information]. All reports will be handled with discretion and confidentiality.

When reporting, please include:
- Your contact information
- Names/usernames of those involved
- Description of the incident
- Any relevant logs or screenshots
- The context and any other relevant details

### Investigation and Response

Upon receiving a report, the maintainers will:

1. **Acknowledge** the report within 48 hours
2. **Investigate** the matter thoroughly and confidentially
3. **Determine** appropriate action based on findings
4. **Communicate** the decision to those involved

Possible actions include:
- Verbal warning
- Required apology
- Temporary suspension
- Permanent removal from community

### Appeal Process

If you believe a decision was made in error, you may appeal within 30 days by providing additional context or evidence.

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for:
- Clarifying standards of acceptable behavior
- Taking appropriate and fair corrective action
- Enforcing this Code of Conduct
- Protecting confidentiality of reporters
- Being fair and impartial

## Expectations

As a member of the gRPC-Web Bridge community, you agree to:
- Treat all community members with respect
- Assume good intentions from others
- Provide constructive feedback only
- Accept criticism gracefully
- Contribute positively to discussions
- Report violations appropriately
- Help maintain a safe community

## Diverse Perspectives

We welcome and encourage diverse perspectives, backgrounds, and experiences. We recognize that:
- Good ideas can come from anywhere
- Different approaches have different strengths
- Respectful disagreement is healthy
- Learning from differences makes us stronger
- Inclusion makes our project better
